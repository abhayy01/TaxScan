1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Authority

The TaxScan AI engine is an automated, client-side tax computation and audit framework designed for Indian taxpayers. It interprets financial statements, Taxpayer Information Summaries (TIS), Annual Information Statements (AIS), Form 16, and Form 26AS to assess statutory return filing mandates and exact net liabilities or refunds.

Statutory Grounding
All computational algorithms strictly abide by the Income Tax Act, 1961, as amended by the Finance Act 2023 and the Finance (No. 2) Act 2024, incorporating official clarification circulars published by the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Ministry of Finance, Government of India.

3. Optimization Decision Rules & Algorithms

The engine generates targeted, personalized recommendations using algorithmic decision rules:

3.1 Regime Arbitrage & Break-Even Deduction Threshold

The engine computes the exact difference: Savings = |Tax_Old - Tax_New|. In Indian taxation, there is a mathematical Break-Even Deduction Point (typically ₹3,75,000 to ₹4,25,000). If total deductions are below this threshold, the Simplified Plan produces a lower tax liability while avoiding capital lock-in in ELSS or PPF.

3.2 Section 80CCD(1B) NPS Headroom Analysis

The engine calculates Headroom = ₹50,000 - Claimed_80CCD1B. If headroom exists, it calculates immediate savings at the taxpayer's marginal rate:
Potential Savings = Headroom × (Marginal Rate × 1.04) (e.g. ₹50,000 in 30% slab yields ₹15,600 tax savings).

3.3 Advance Tax Penal Interest Avoidance (Sections 208, 234B & 234C)

Under Section 208, advance tax is mandatory if net tax payable after TDS exceeds ₹10,000. If unpaid, Section 234C levies 1% per month on quarterly installment deficits (15% June, 45% Sept, 75% Dec, 100% March), and Section 234B levies 1% per month from April 1 if less than 90% was paid. The app alerts users whenever net payable exceeds ₹10,000.

4. Coordinate-Aware PDF Parsing Mechanics

Standard PDF text extractors join strings sequentially, scrambling multi-column tables. TaxScan AI implements geometric coordinate sorting:

  1. Sorts text tokens descending by vertical coordinate Y (top of page to bottom).
  2. Sorts tokens on the same horizontal line (ΔY ≤ 4 pt) ascending by coordinate X (left to right).
  3. Reconstructs table rows into contiguous strings containing Category, TIS Code, and Financial Values.
  4. Prioritizes the official, verified Processed Value over Reported Value in TIS table rows.
  5. Provides a fallback candidate detector that identifies all monetary figures (> ₹500) for one-click manual assignment.

5. Mandatory Return Filing Criteria — Section 139(1)

Under Section 139(1) (including the Seventh Proviso), return filing is legally mandatory if:

  • Gross Total Income exceeds the basic exemption limit (₹2.5L / ₹3.0L).
  • Total TDS / TCS deducted in the year is ₹25,000 or more (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
  • Electricity consumption expenditure exceeds ₹1,00,000 during the financial year.
  • Expenditure on foreign travel exceeds ₹2,00,000 for self or any other person.
  • Aggregate deposits in current bank accounts exceed ₹50,00,000.
  • Business turnover exceeds ₹60,00,000 or professional receipts exceed ₹10,00,000.
  • The taxpayer holds foreign assets, bank accounts, or signing authority abroad.
  • The entity is a Company or LLP (mandatory regardless of profit or loss).
